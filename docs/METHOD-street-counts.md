# Street counts: wrong-way riding and people crossing on foot (draft method, not live)

Drafted 3 Nov 2026. This is a proposal only. Nothing collected under it is published until Rohit approves the method and the first counts have been checked against a second counter.

## Why this exists

arcroad says nothing about walking, and it does not count wrong-way riding at any place (AGENTS.md). Open map data cannot show either one. If arcroad is ever to say "people cross on foot here" or "riders go the wrong way here", the evidence has to be counted on the ground, the same way the detours were driven.

## The count

- **Who.** Two volunteers at the same spot and the same time, each counting on their own. Counts that disagree by more than 15% are not used.
- **When.** 15-minute blocks, at least two blocks per place: one weekday peak (08:30–10:30 or 17:30–19:30) and one off-peak. The day and times are recorded.
- **What is counted.** Only these, by mode (two-wheeler, three-wheeler, car, bus/truck), with nothing else recorded:
  - (a) vehicles travelling against the marked direction on a carriageway or service road within 100 m of the place;
  - (b) people crossing the carriageway on foot within 100 m of the place.
- **Never recorded.** No photos of people or number plates, and no names. A tally sheet only.
- **Volunteer safety.** Counters stand on the footpath, never on the carriageway or median, and only in daylight.

## How it may appear on the site

- As its own labelled layer, "Counted by volunteers", separate from every driven or modelled figure, with the date and count blocks shown.
- As a rate per 15 minutes, never as a cause of any crash. arcroad never links a death or injury to a single road feature.
- Grade: a citizen count (not A or B evidence). It can support a foot-overbridge or underpass option only when at least two independent count days agree.

## Hypothesis and competing explanation (rule 5), fixed before any count

- **H.** Where the legal U-turn is far, more vehicles travel the wrong way near the place than at control spots on the same road where a legal U-turn is near.
- **C.** Wrong-way rates reflect general traffic volume and enforcement, not U-turn distance.
- **What separates them.** Wrong-way counts per 1,000 vehicles in the right direction, compared between each place and a control spot 300–500 m away near an existing opening, on the same day.
