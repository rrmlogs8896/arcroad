# Pre-registration: fixed structures in road medians (structures-v1)

Written 4 Nov 2026, before any data was fetched. Changes are dated below and made before a run, never after.

## Question
Where do fixed structures stand in the median of a divided main road in Hyderabad, and do arcroad's places with one nearby have longer detours than places without?

## What counts
Any mapped structure between the two carriageways of a one-way-pair main road (motorway, trunk, primary, secondary), each carriageway within 30 m and running in the opposite direction: places of worship of every faith, memorials and wayside shrines, statues, police posts, transformers and substations. Every kind is detected by the same rule. Source: OpenStreetMap via Overpass (ODbL).

## Neutrality and safety, fixed now
- Every faith and every kind of structure is treated identically; no kind is singled out in code, counts or wording.
- The public site may show only the total of all kinds together, and only as a reason openings are constrained. The breakdown by kind, the list of locations and any photographs stay internal.
- No individual place of worship is ever named, mapped or pictured on the site, in outreach or in a letter.
- Nothing claims that a structure caused a crash, a death or a detour. A structure is a constraint on where an opening can go, not a cause.
- Rohit's own photographs of such places stay private and are never used to identify them.

## Comparison and gate
Places with at least one structure within 500 m versus places with none. Median of the mean modelled detour in each group. No comparison is reported if either group has fewer than 4 places. A difference is reported as "a pattern worth checking on the ground", never as an effect, until drives at both kinds of place confirm it.

## Known limitations
- OpenStreetMap misses many small structures, especially unmapped shrines; absence in the data is not absence on the road.
- A 30 m rule can miss very wide medians and can catch structures on narrow traffic islands.
