# Field Day 1: route sheet (west corridor, Patancheru end to Miyapur)

Driver-facing. Planned for Sunday 1 November 2026.

This sheet holds stop ids, the public side-A coordinates from
`data/field/field_day_1_stops.json` and generic instructions, nothing else. It
deliberately shows **no expected distances of any kind**: a driver who knows
what the model expects can be steered towards it, and then the drive proves
nothing (STATE.md, Phase 2b). Drive the road as you find it.

Sequence generated by:

```
python tools/field_plan.py data/field/field_day_1_stops.json --start S04 --end S13 --split 12
```

(nearest neighbour then 2-opt on straight-line distance between side-A points,
fixed start at the west end and fixed finish at the Miyapur end).

---

## Before the ride (checklist)

**Logger (the 29 Oct recce showed why this comes first)**

- [ ] Use a logger that writes a point **every second, including while standing
      still**. OsmAnd trip recording: logging interval 1 s, minimum displacement
      0, minimum speed 0, minimum accuracy off (or the nearest equivalents). A
      logger with a distance filter stops writing at a red light, and a hole over
      15 s between A and B makes the stop unscorable.
- [ ] Two-minute test at home: record, stand still for 60 s, ride or walk a
      little, export the GPX and run `python tools/track_quality.py test.gpx`.
      Pass: `median_step_s 1` and `holes_over_limit 0`, with no ADVICE lines.
- [ ] iPhone: Location for the logger set to Always with Precise Location on;
      Low Power Mode off; Auto-Lock off while recording (screen on); notifications
      silenced. Keep the logger app in the foreground the whole time.
- [ ] Start recording before leaving and do not stop it between stops. One
      continuous track for the day (or one per leg).

**Rider and machine**

- [ ] Phone on a firm handlebar mount with a clear view of the sky. Never handle
      the phone while moving; pull over safely to check anything.
- [ ] Phone charged to 100%, power bank and cable connected on the mount.
- [ ] Helmet (and pillion helmet), fuel for 160 km, documents (licence, RC,
      insurance, PUC).
- [ ] Daylight start, around 06:30 to 07:00, for low traffic and visible signs.
      If any part runs after dark: headlight and tail light checked, reflective
      layer, and no crossings attempted where you cannot read the signs.
- [ ] Water, a printed or offline copy of this sheet, and a way to note times
      (a paper log or voice memo while parked).

**Legal compliance, every stop**

- [ ] Only legal manoeuvres: permitted U-turns, signed median openings, flyover
      or underpass loops, junctions. Never ride against traffic, never use a
      footpath, never cross a median, never take a "no U-turn" opening.
- [ ] Never stop in a live lane. On NH-65 and other highways, pause only on the
      shoulder or a service road, or simply roll through.
- [ ] If no legal crossing is found within 30 minutes, note it and move on. That
      is a result too.

## The crossing protocol (every stop marked "crossing")

Each stop has two sides: **A** (the pin in the table) and **B**, the opposite
carriageway, 12 to 60 m away across the median. The table says which compass
side of A it lies on.

1. **Navigate to side A** by any means. Approach it on the A carriageway, in the
   direction that carriageway's traffic flows.
2. **Register A:** be within 40 m of the pin for at least 3 consecutive logged
   samples. Rolling through at 20 km/h or less does this (about 7 s inside the
   circle). Stop only if it is safe and legal to do so.
3. **Mute navigation and cross legally:** continue in the flow of traffic to the
   nearest permitted U-turn, median opening or junction **as a local driver
   would find it from the road**, using signs and what you can see, not an app's
   suggestion. Then come back along the B carriageway.
4. **Register B:** within 40 m of the B side for at least 3 samples, rolling or
   safely stopped.
5. **All within 30 minutes** of leaving A, and in the order **A then B**. B
   then A does not count (the recce on 29 Oct visited S04 in that order), nor
   does a crossing made earlier and completed later.
6. Note the time at A and at B, plus anything unusual: an opening that is not
   where you expected, a barricade, a closed or newly opened U-turn, works.
7. Move to the next stop. Do not repeat a stop to "get a better number".

**Photo target (T1):** pull over safely near the pin, photograph the median
opening from the roadside in daylight (road and opening only; avoid faces and
number plates), and move on.

Do the stops in the order below. If time runs out, stop **at a point in the
sequence** and leave the rest for another day; do not pick and choose stops.
Skipped stops stay in the count as not driven.

## Time and distance (estimates, not measurements)

Straight-line distance between side-A points times a road factor of 1.4, plus a
flat allowance of 1 to 3 km and 8 minutes per crossing and 5 minutes for the
photo target, at an average of 25 km/h. None of these figures comes from the
model's routing.

| | Stops | Road km between stops | Total km | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leg 1 | 12 (11 crossings, 1 photo) | about 30 | 41 to 63 | 3.2 to 4.1 |
| Transfer S09 to S24 | | about 14 | 14 | 0.6 |
| Leg 2 | 14 crossings | about 38 | 52 to 80 | 4.0 to 5.1 |
| **Whole day** | **26** | **about 82** | **107 to 157** | **7.7 to 9.7** |

Plus the ride to S04 and home from S13, and breaks. The whole sequence is a
long day on a two-wheeler. **Recommended: ride Leg 1 on Sunday, and Leg 2 the
same day only if you are fresh after a proper break; otherwise on a second
outing.** Either way, follow the order.

## After the ride

1. Stop recording and export the GPX. Keep it on your own machine: raw tracks
   never enter this repository (`*.gpx` is gitignored) and are never shared.
2. Check the track: `python tools/track_quality.py TRACK.gpx`
3. Score it:

   ```
   python tools/score_drive.py TRACK.gpx data/field/field_day_1_stops.json --network model_network.csv
   ```

   `model_network.csv` comes from `tools/export_network.py` (see STATE.md) and
   is written to `out/model_network.csv` (pass that path to `--network`),
   never next to this sheet.
4. Only the per-stop numbers leave the scoring step. Nothing is published until
   the results have been reviewed against the gate in `pipeline/groundtruth.py`.
