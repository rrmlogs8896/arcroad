Accessibility

Everything shown in a map or 3D view is also given as text: the globe has a list of every place, the drive has its facts below it. The site works by keyboard and respects reduced motion. Tell us if something does not work for you.

Privacy

arcroad sets no cookies, runs no analytics and stores nothing you type. Letters and reports open in your own email or WhatsApp; we see them only if you send them to us. The globe loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which, like any website, sees your IP address.

In the arcroad iPhone app, your location is used only while you record a crossing. The track stays on your phone, is deleted once the crossing is measured, and is never sent anywhere. Only a summary you choose to share leaves the phone. The app has no account, no analytics and no advertising.
