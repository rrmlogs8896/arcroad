Change log

What changed

Every update to arcroad, newest first. When something was wrong, it is listed here with the fix, not quietly replaced.

Week of 23 November

Recognition, joining in, a calmer page

  • AddedSecond prize in the Citizens Innovation Challenge at Cyberabad Moves 2026.
  • AddedJoining takes one field and one tap from any page, then one more tap to say how you would like to help. No name, phone or email.
  • ChangedLong explanations now sit under a quiet “+”, so each page leads with what matters. Credits, licences, Privacy and Terms stay fully open.
  • AddedShare buttons, a clearer roadmap, a page on how arcroad is built, and sources behind more of our claims.
  • Held backA stricter rule for reading phone tracks was tested and not adopted: it also rejected genuine crossings. Published results are unchanged.
  • PrivacyNo drive days or times of day appear anywhere public.
  • CorrectedHome page answers that still described the project as undriven now reflect the driven results, and the privacy answer now says plainly what joining and the report form ask for.

Week of 16 November

Tighter claims

  • ChangedThe headline now leads with the strict count, judged only under rules fixed before driving.
  • AddedTerms and Privacy pages, and a short, accurate description for every page.

Week of 9 November

Rules written before results

  • AddedThe Hi-Tech City one-way loop study was registered in writing before any distance was computed, and every later change to it is dated.
  • AddedRules for reading road-side photos were written before labelling began.
  • CorrectedThe first photo-label check failed. It was redone with written definitions on a fresh sample, and no rate from photos is reported.
  • CorrectedPlaces on the Outer Ring Road were taken out of every count: it is a motorway, with no at-grade turns.
  • ChangedA clearer phone menu, and an independent route check that now reports every outcome, including disagreements.

Week of 2 November

arcroad goes public

  • AddedThe home page and the driven results, place by place and direction by direction.
  • AddedThe cost calculator, what else the road costs, a report form and a way to write to the people responsible.
  • AddedDrive it: the detour as a map for every phone, and as a 3D ride.
  • AddedEvidence levels from 1 to 4 for every place and direction, with how much each source can be trusted measured on control places.
  • CorrectedRepeat counts now count only the same direction. One place went back to candidate as a result.
  • CorrectedKPHB Colony now carries its local name. Faint text raised to meet accessibility contrast.

25 October to 1 November

The start

  • AddedA detour model built from open map data, with tests, and the same counts collected for 29 Indian cities.
  • AddedThe rule every drive is judged by: a pass must actually reach both points, not just come near one.
