Whose hour counts?

Standard transport appraisal values travel time at wage rates. Applied here, an hour lost by a software engineer in Gachibowli is worth many times an hour lost by a construction worker in Pashamylaram, and the arithmetic then concludes the richer corridor deserves investment first. That is one way peripheral settlements end up last in the queue.

This page refuses to pick for you. Set the hourly value to zero and you see fuel and wear alone. Set it to a wage and you see the conventional appraisal figure. The gap between those two numbers is itself worth looking at.

What this is not

It is not a measurement. It is arithmetic on assumptions you chose, and it is only as strong as the weakest one: crossings per day, for which no public dataset exists for this city. Treat the single-person figure as directional and any group figure as an order of magnitude.

It leaves out tolls, congestion, roadworks, crashes and health damage, and it never puts a price on injury or death. It makes no claim about anyone's competence or motives.

Where the numbers come from
Extra distance and speed: arcroad's own drives, 1 Nov 2026
Four city passes that matched the model: 1.53 to 2.28 km beyond the straight line, 3 to 12 minutes each. The Outer Ring Road pass is left out because turning round there needs an interchange by design. Every pass.
Fuel prices, Hyderabad: petrol ₹116.15/L (16 Oct 2026); diesel ₹103.82/L and CNG ₹97/kg (25 Oct 2026, indicative)
Refreshed from the oil marketing companies' published rates before each release.
Tax inside the price: Telangana VAT 35.2% on petrol and 27% on diesel; central excise ₹21.90/L petrol and ₹17.80/L diesel
VAT rates set by the state in Feb 2015 and unchanged since, as widely reported. Excise from the oil companies' price build-up of Apr 2025, not yet re-verified for Oct 2026. VAT is levied on the price before VAT, so its share of the pump price is VAT ÷ (100 + VAT). CNG and electricity taxes are not modelled.
Value of time: about ₹50 to ₹600 an hour in Indian studies
A Delhi congestion-cost study (values for 2013, arXiv 1708.08984) and a Bengaluru congestion-pricing field experiment (Kreindler, J-PAL and IGC). Peer-reviewed or working papers; no single figure is official.
Fleet mix: about 73% two-wheelers
Telangana Transport Department figures as reported in May 2023: 1.13 crore two-wheelers of 1.54 crore vehicles statewide. Registrations are not road use.
Fuel economy: 50 km/L two-wheeler, 16 km/L petrol car, 20 km/L diesel car, 28 km/kg CNG auto, 40 km/kWh e-scooter, 6 km/kWh e-car
Typical real-world figures for the Indian fleet rather than manufacturer claims. Every one is adjustable.
Tailpipe carbon: 2.31 kg CO₂/L petrol, 2.68 diesel, 2.75 per kg CNG
Standard combustion factors. Electric vehicles show zero at the tailpipe, which is not zero emissions: grid generation is excluded.
India's GDP per person: ₹2,43,803 (FY2025-26, current prices)
Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation, with the IMF World Economic Outlook (Apr 2026) for the dollar and PPP figures.
Telangana: ₹4,20,347 GSDP per person (2024-25); GSDP ₹17,82,004 crore (2025-26, revised estimate)
PRS Legislative Research, Telangana Budget Analysis 2026-27, from the state's budget documents.
