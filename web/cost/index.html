What crossing your own road costs: you, the city, the state.

When a median gap is closed, or a barricade goes up, people drive round. This page turns that extra distance and time into rupees: fuel, the tax inside that fuel, and time. Then it scales up to the city and sets the total beside Telangana's economy. It is an estimated cost to people, not a "GDP loss": GDP counts what is produced, not time wasted.

  • Brent crude does not enter this arithmetic. A change in Brent reaches you only through the pump price, so the pump price is the input. Brent is shown on the site only once it can be taken daily from a public-domain series (US EIA).
  • Tolls, wear on the vehicle, extra emissions and the safety cost of U-turns are not included yet, so these totals are on the low side.
  • Diesel (27% VAT) and CNG vehicles are not modelled yet.
