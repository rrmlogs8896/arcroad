arcroad · open data · ODbL

Open data, free to use.

What the open map records for each city: roads, divided main roads, pedestrian crossings and footbridges, with the population living there. Download it, check it, build on it.

The first release is being collected now: the 8 largest Indian cities, then a world sample. It will appear here once checked.

What these numbers are, and are not
  • Counts of what OpenStreetMap records inside an approximate box around each city, with GHSL 2020 population for the same box.
  • They show how well the map records a city as much as the city itself: a city with few mapped crossings may simply be under-mapped. That is where volunteers help most.
  • Not an official boundary, not a measurement of the ground, and not a ranking of cities.
  • Detour figures are published only for places checked on the ground. See driven results.
