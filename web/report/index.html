Photos, safety and privacy
  • Attach a photo in your mail app if you have one. No faces or number plates: blur them first. Raw photos are never published.
  • Never use the phone while driving, and never stop on the carriageway to take a photo.
  • Nothing you type here is stored by arcroad. It goes only to the address you send it to, from your own email.
