arcroad · privacy

Privacy

What this site knows about you, in plain words. Checked against the site's code on 18 November 2026.

No cookies, no analytics

arcroad sets no cookies, runs no analytics and keeps no record of who visits. Nothing is stored in your browser.

Nothing you type is kept

The cost calculator, the letter kit and the report form work inside your own browser. Letters and reports open in your own email or WhatsApp, and we see them only if you send them to us.

Hosting and map tiles

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, which handles basic request details such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site. The globe loads map tiles from OpenFreeMap, which, like any website, sees your IP address.

If you email us

Emails to hello@arcroad.org are kept only to reply and to fix what you report. Ask, and we will delete them.

Volunteers and photos

Your name is never published unless you ask for credit. Faces and number plates are blurred before anything is published, and raw photos stay private.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at hello@arcroad.org. If this changes, this page and its date change first.
