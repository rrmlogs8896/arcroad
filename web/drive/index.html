How this is built, and its limits

The distances and the clock are from our drive on 1 Nov 2026: one driver, one car, phone GPS once a second (about ±50 m). The clock runs in the real time of that pass, replayed faster. Where the car slows and stops along the way is illustrated; the total time is what we measured. Another day, or another hour, will differ.

The road is drawn straight and to scale in length; the real road bends a little and its exact U-turn points may sit a little nearer or further. The vehicles, buildings and trees are made up for illustration, and the traffic is drawn to match the average speed we measured on each pass (about 29 km/h one way, 8 km/h the other). No photo, person or number plate from the drive appears here.

The monthly figures assume one crossing each way on every working day (22 days) and use the cost calculator's own figures: petrol at ₹116.15/L (Hyderabad, 16 Oct 2026), 50 km/L for a two-wheeler and 16 km/L for a car. They count fuel only. This page says nothing about walking: arcroad measures driving.
