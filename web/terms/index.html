Terms of use

Plain rules for using this site. Last updated 18 November 2026.

Information, not advice

arcroad measures what road layout costs people, from open data and our own drives. Nothing here is legal, engineering, safety or financial advice, and a computed result is not an official finding. Modelled figures are candidates until driven. Never use your phone while driving.

Figures can change

Maps change, roads change and we correct mistakes in the open. Each page says when its figures were checked. If you find an error, tell us and we will fix it and say so.
