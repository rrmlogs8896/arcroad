What this measures, and what it does not

  • Distance only, never travel time, by the shortest legal driving route on the open map.
  • Driving only. The open map holds no usable walking network for Hyderabad yet.
  • Squares, never homes. People per square come from an open population estimate, so no one is located.
  • Government and private hospitals are kept apart. A private hospital next door does not serve someone who depends on free government care.
  • Squares the map cannot reach by road are shown hatched and counted, never left out.
  • A high figure is a place to check on the ground, not a finding, until it passes verification.
