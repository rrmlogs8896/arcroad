What you may reuse

The numbers are open so that anyone can check them. The name and the work around them are not.

Free to reuse, with credit

The measurements and downloads are under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), as OpenStreetMap requires. The code is under the MIT licence. Credit: "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Measurements by arcroad (ODbL)." Anything you build from the data must stay open under the same licence. Every published file carries a build mark and is recorded on the day it ships, so a copy without credit can be traced to its source.

The arcroad name and logo, the films and illustrations, the page design and the wording of these pages. © 2026 arcroad. All rights reserved. Quoting a sentence with a link back is fine.

Copies and look-alikes

Do not present a copy of this site, or a site built from its data, as arcroad, or suggest arcroad endorses it. A mirror or rebuild must say clearly that it is not arcroad and link to arcroad.org.
